How the same dataset moves through all four tools

Excel: profile the CSVs, convert amounts with dim_fx_rate and build the first KPIs with pivots; the starter workbook (below) does it on a merchant-month summary already in USD.
Tableau & Power BI: don't connect to the CSV files. Load all 18 tables into MySQL, then connect to SQL: vw_payment, vw_confirmed_fraud, vw_refund_usd and the merchant-month mart.

Data Model

18 tables, one hub: fact_payment in the middle

fact_payment (one row per attempt) sits in the centre with its dimensions: merchant, customer, payment method, status, date and the FX rate on (date, currency). Refunds, disputes, fraud cases and risk scores hang off payment_id — all one-to-many except the risk score — and settlements hang off merchant, with their ledger lines below. Watch out for this

Common QA mistakes on this project's KPIs

Before you sign off on any KPI card or chart, check for these

  • Every amount in USD via dim_fx_rate on (transaction_date_key, currency_code) — never SUM(transaction_amount).
  • Attempts ≠ intents: label every count; success rate excludes Pending.
  • Active merchant = ≥1 successful payment, not the status column.
  • Confirmed fraud once per payment (vw_confirmed_fraud), suspected risk on its own card.
  • Chargeback = lost / accepted disputes only; refunds, disputes and fraud never added together.
  • Aggregate refunds, cases and lines first, then join — check row counts before and after.
  • Reconcile every KPI against the equivalent SQL query before presenting.
